# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP + ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion. Chosen by Harish's brief (GSAP and Framer Motion are required); Next.js delegated to the build.

## Users

Recruiters and engineers screening candidates for backend, AI/ML and cloud internships and graduate roles. They arrive from a resume or LinkedIn link, usually on a laptop, and give the page a short first look before deciding whether to read further or get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Harish Ram S, a pre-final year B.E. Computer Science and Engineering student at Chennai Institute of Technology (2024–2028). It shows who he is, what he has built, and gives a direct way to contact him. Success is a visitor who remembers him and reaches out.

## Positioning

A portfolio told as an interactive documentary: the visitor literally shines a light on the subject. Backend and systems work is explained through authored diagrams of how each system works, not screenshots of interfaces.

## Operating Context

Single-page site, deployed on Vercel from `github.com/harish-ram247/My-Portfolio`. Linked from the resume, GitHub profile and LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary projects: TRACE, CyberShield, Industrial IoT Predictive Maintenance.
- Secondary projects: Kubernetes monitoring with Prometheus and Grafana, Urban Farm Management System, Pocketwise.
- No other projects are shown. AI Fitness & Nutrition Assistant is excluded.
- The resume is the source of truth. No invented facts, statistics, links or screenshots.
- Undecided: whether to show the phone number, whether old-site items missing from the resume return.

## Brand Commitments

- Near-black ground, warm ivory type, controlled yellow and red, occasional blue; one ivory (#EAE8E2) section for the collaborate scene.
- Oversized and outlined display type, mono captions, thin rules, annotations, film grain, portrait cutout.
- Opening composition follows Harish's supplied reference image.
- No pyramids, floating cubes, generic geometric blocks, random cards or heavy glassmorphism.
- The portrait is never altered in identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume PDF (`public/Harish-Ram-S-Resume.pdf`).
- Portrait (`Professional photo  Harish Ram S.jpeg`, cutout in `public/portrait/`).
- Public repos: TRACE, Expense_Tracker (Pocketwise), Urban-farm.
- Absent, never to be fabricated: screenshots, demos or repos for CyberShield and Industrial IoT; live links for any project.

## Product Principles

1. Every fact traces to the resume, GitHub or Harish.
2. The person and the work lead; the interface recedes.
3. Explain how a system works rather than decorate around it.
4. Motion serves the story and never blocks reading, scrolling or contact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operable, visible focus, readable contrast, full content available with reduced motion and without a pointer.
